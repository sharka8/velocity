PRACTICE

Practice at Home

Safety: Use a safe open space and get an adult's permission before serving or hitting toward a wall. Stay away from people, windows, cars, and breakable objects.

TEAMWORK + CHARACTER

Be a Great Teammate

🗣 Communicate

Call “Mine!” early and use clear, positive words.

🙌 Encourage

Celebrate effort. Help teammates get ready for the next ball after mistakes.

🔄 Reset

Everyone makes mistakes. Learn, say “next ball,” and return to ready position.

👂 Be Coachable

Listen to feedback, try the correction, and ask respectful questions.

🤝 Show Respect

Respect teammates, opponents, coaches, officials, and facilities.

🏐 Put the Team First

Communicate, cover teammates, and stay engaged even when you don't touch the ball.

Quick Teammate Check

Velocity Teammate Promise

I will play hard, communicate, encourage my teammates, listen to my coaches, respect opponents and officials, and get ready for the next ball after mistakes.
