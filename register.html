Fall Season II Registration

Register for Vail Velocity

Fall Season II runs October 19–December 12, 2026. Our coed league welcomes players ages 9–15 of all experience levels, with divisions formed by age and skill level. Most weeks may include up to two practices and one game, depending on gym availability. There are no games during Thanksgiving week. Standard registration is $135 and includes a game jersey.

Season Summary

Before You Register

  • Fee: $135 per player
  • Included: Game jersey
  • Dates: October 19–December 12, 2026
  • Ages: 9–15
  • League: Coed — boys and girls welcome
  • Skill Level: All experience levels welcome
  • Divisions: Players grouped by age and skill level
  • Schedule: Most weeks may include up to two practices and one game, depending on gym availability
  • Thanksgiving: No games during Thanksgiving week
  • Locations: Practice and game locations are assigned based on enrollment and facility availability

Players are grouped by age, experience and skill level to create balanced teams and appropriate competition. Practice locations are assigned based on enrollment, facility availability and family location when possible.

Divisions by Age & Skill Level

Velocity Volleyball forms divisions based on age, experience, demonstrated skill and registration numbers. This helps us create balanced teams and appropriate competition for players at different stages of development.

Beginners are always welcome. Final division and team placement is determined by Velocity Volleyball.

Volunteer Coaching

Help Build Vail Velocity

Parents selected to serve as a Head Coach will receive one complimentary player registration for a child participating during the same season they coach. Parents selected to serve as an Assistant Coach will receive 50% off one player registration for the same season.

Previous volleyball coaching experience is not required. All selected coaches must complete the Vail Velocity Coach Academy, a virtual online training, before the season begins. Approved coaches may also be required to complete volunteer paperwork or background screening.

Only complete the questions below if you are interested in coaching.

Policies and Agreements

Practice Placement Notice

We will use school, district, closest-school and family-location information to help create convenient practice groups whenever facility and team availability allow. Practice location, teammate, coach and division requests are not guaranteed.

Refund Policy

A full refund is available before the official season begins. After the official season begins, refunds are limited to 50% of the registration fee actually paid. Any payment-processing fees charged by an outside provider may be nonrefundable.
