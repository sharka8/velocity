Why Vail Velocity

Built to give young athletes more time to learn and play.

We created Vail Velocity because every child deserves the opportunity to learn volleyball in a positive, encouraging environment. Our goal is to help young athletes develop skills, confidence, teamwork and sportsmanship while having fun and building friendships.

We aim to provide each team with space for two practices and one game per week, giving players meaningful repetition and consistent opportunities to improve.

Volunteer parents make it possible.

Vail Velocity welcomes parents who are willing to lead and encourage young athletes. Previous volleyball coaching experience is not required.

Selected coaches complete the Vail Velocity Coach Academy, a virtual online training that covers league values, safety, fundamentals, rotations, sportsmanship and respectful communication with officials.

Coach Information
Frequently asked questions

What families should know.

Does my child need volleyball experience?

No. Beginners are welcome, and experienced players will still have room to improve.

How often will teams practice and play?

Our goal is to provide space for two practices and one game per week for each team, subject to final gym availability.

How much does registration cost?

Registration is $135 per player and includes a game jersey.

Where will practices and games be held?

The league will use available Vail and Sunnyside school-district facilities. We will use the school closest to each player's home to help create convenient practice groups whenever possible.

How are volunteer coaches prepared?

Selected coaches complete the Vail Velocity Coach Academy, a virtual online training covering league expectations, sportsmanship, safety, volleyball fundamentals and working respectfully with referees.
