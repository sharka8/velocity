Why Velocity

More court time. More growth.

Vail Velocity is designed to give young athletes meaningful repetition, supportive competition and coaches who emphasize development over the scoreboard.

Learn

Beginners are welcome, and experienced players still have room to improve through consistent practice.

Compete

Weekly games give players a chance to apply skills while learning teamwork, resilience and sportsmanship.

Belong

We want every player to feel part of a team, build friendships and enjoy coming back to the gym.

Volunteer coaches needed

Help local kids learn the game.

No previous coaching experience is required. Approved head coaches receive one complimentary player registration for the season they coach and complete the online Vail Velocity Coach Academy.

We provide the training.

Coach Academy covers league expectations, safety, fundamentals, rotations, sportsmanship and communication with officials.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

Does my child need volleyball experience?

No. Beginners are welcome, and experienced players will still have opportunities to improve.

How often will teams practice and play?

Most weeks include up to two practices and one game, depending on facility availability. There are no games Thanksgiving week.

How much is Vail/Rita Ranch registration?

Vail/Rita Ranch registration is $135 per player and includes a game jersey.

Where will practices and games be held?

Teams use available school facilities in the Vail and Sunnyside Unified School District areas. We try to create convenient practice groups whenever possible.

How are coaches prepared?

Selected coaches complete the Vail Velocity Coach Academy before the season begins.
