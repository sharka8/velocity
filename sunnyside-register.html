Register for Velocity Volleyball

Youth volleyball for Sunnyside Unified School District students. Registration is $50 per player.

Season summary

Before you register

  • Fee: $50 per player
  • Program: Velocity Volleyball
  • Season: Seven weeks
  • Weekly goal: Two practices and one game
  • Locations: Sunnyside-area school facilities
  • Divisions: Planned ages 9–11 and 12–15

This registration is for students attending Sunnyside Unified School District schools. Practice and game locations will be assigned based on facility, coach, age-group and enrollment availability.

Policies and agreements

Sunnyside Unified School District disclaimer

Sunnyside Unified School District is neither endorsing nor promoting this program. Distribution of program information is provided as a public service to families. District resources were not used to produce this registration page.

Sunnyside program eligibility and placement

This registration is intended for students attending Sunnyside Unified School District schools. Practice location, teammate, coach, and school-placement requests are considered but are not guaranteed.

Refund policy

A full refund is available before the official season begins. After the official season begins, refunds are limited to 50% of the $50 registration fee. Any payment-processing fees charged by an outside provider may be nonrefundable.
